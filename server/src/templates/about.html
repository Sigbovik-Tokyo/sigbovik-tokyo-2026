What is Sigbovik?

Sigbovik is a hilariously satirical conference that presents research, presentations, and talks on niche, unprofessional topics that are usually neglected by mainstream media and science. Originally an yearly conference held at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), Sigbovik Tokyo aims to capture the idea, culture, and bring the tradition to Tokyo!

Organizers

This year's Sigbovik Tokyo was organized and hosted by a list of student organizations and people. This section is dedicated to honor them all.

  • Tokyo Student Network (TSN) is a student-driven community connecting students across universities in Tokyo. We create a safe, inclusive space to collaborate, learn, and grow. Tokyo Student Network Homepage
  • Temple University Japan (TUJ) CS Society is a student-led organisation connecting Computer Science students from Temple University to other universities and opportunities in Japan, hosting hackathons and other events for university students. TUJ CS Society Blog
  • Google Developer Groups on Campus IPUT is an independently run developer community based at the International Professional University of Technology (IPUT)'s three campuses in Tokyo, Osaka, and Nagoya. GDGoC IPUT's Homepage
  • Google Developer Groups on Campus Waseda University is one of Japan’s largest student-led developer communities, bringing together a diverse bilingual network of students to collaborate, innovate, and build impactful projects. GDGoC Waseda's Homepage
